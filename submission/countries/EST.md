# Estonia (EST)

2020–2024 pathway: insufficient data.

Available Ember years: 2020,2021,2022,2023,2024,2025. Core research market: False.

Clean generation change: +17.5%; coal generation change: not reported; demand change: -5.6% (2020–2024).

Prioritize demand, clean/fossil additions, net imports and emissions. Add coal comparisons only where EI reports both endpoints; avoid percentage changes from zero bases.

## Recommended charts

- Demand-contraction panel: do not report a clean-growth/demand-growth ratio when demand fell.
- Net imports as a share of demand: trade is material (>5% absolute in 2024).
- Annual clean/fossil additions plus demand change and net imports (Ember accounting identity).
- Power emissions and carbon intensity in separate panels or clearly labelled 2020 indices.
